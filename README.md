**How the app picks players**

Each time you press Select 4 Players, the app looks at every possible game and picks the fairest one, using these rules in order of importance:

New partners first. It avoids pairing people who have already played together, until everyone has partnered everyone else.
Fewer games first. Players who have played less get picked, so nobody sits out too much.
Even teams. It tries to make the two teams equally strong, based on past results.
Mix up opponents. It slightly prefers opponents who haven’t played against each other much.
A little randomness, so it doesn’t repeat the same choice.

Ratings: everyone starts equal. Winning raises your rating and losing lowers it, and beating a stronger team earns more points.

Rotation reset: once everyone has partnered everyone, the partner history clears and a new rotation starts. Ratings and wins are kept.
